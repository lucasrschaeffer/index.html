Our Mission

Building Stronger Communities Through Youth Leadership

At Pathfinders, we believe that young people have the power to drive meaningful change. Our program connects motivated volunteers with real opportunities to serve, learn, and lead in their neighborhoods.

We partner with nonprofits, local government, and community organizations to create projects that address actual community needs — not just check a box.
