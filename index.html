Our Values

What Drives Us

Pathfinders NYC believes that every young person has the potential to become a leader and changemaker in their community.

Our mission is to provide comprehensive leadership development and meaningful volunteer opportunities that empower youth to:

🔥

Discover Their Purpose

Explore your passions and find your voice as a young leader in NYC.

🌱

Build Essential Skills

Master communication, collaboration, and critical thinking through hands-on experience.

🎯

Drive Real Change

Lead projects that create tangible, positive impact in neighborhoods across the city.

75%
Of our alumni report increased confidence in leadership abilities Source: Pathfinders NYC Impact Survey 2024

By partnering with schools, nonprofits, and government agencies, we create pathways for youth to make meaningful contributions to their communities while building the skills and confidence they need to succeed.
